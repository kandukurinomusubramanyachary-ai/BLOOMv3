# Founder device acceptance

Use disposable test accounts and synthetic records. Record build ID/commit, device model, OS, date and PASS/FAIL with a short description. Keep health information, passwords, camera frames and tokens out of GitHub reports. Public release remains blocked until these checks and the separate legal/service approvals pass.

1. **Install/update and startup.** Install the provided signed Android APK over the prior Bloom beta. Open it, close it and reopen. Expected: no crash or blank screen; existing records remain. iOS requires an approved signing setup before an installable artifact is available.
2. **Signup and restoration.** Create a disposable account, complete onboarding, close Bloom fully and reopen. Expected: the same account is signed in and the completed onboarding is retained. Request password recovery from the login screen. Expected: a recovery email arrives at the correct mailbox without exposing account data.
3. **Today and Timeline.** Enter a synthetic check-in and a period date. Return to Timeline, switch Month/Year, then restart. Expected: the intended dates and entries remain, with no duplicate check-in. Cycle estimates remain estimates.
4. **Offline and account switch.** View saved records offline, reconnect, then sign out and sign into a different disposable account. Expected: the first account’s records and Meg conversations are absent from the second account. No queued first-account write appears in the second account.
5. **Meg — after provider/service setup.** Send a synthetic greeting, verify an actual provider reply, retry after a connection interruption, switch conversations and restart. Expected: one user message and one reply per request; history and helpful/unhelpful feedback (including removal) persist. Missing provider configuration should show a recoverable error, never a fabricated successful reply.
6. **Memory privacy.** Turn Meg memory off, start a different conversation and check that previous-conversation details are not recalled from memory. Expected: the current conversation still has context, while cross-conversation memory is off. Existing records are not represented as deleted just by turning the switch off.
7. **Guided Strength.** Choose a workout, select Continue guided, pause during a set, background/reopen, rest, stop early and save. Expected: no camera prompt; paused time does not generate reps; history and reports label the session as paced/guided. Saved completed movements survive leaving the workout.
8. **Native camera QA — internal native-qa artifact only.** Select a supported squat, wall push-up or standing side-leg raise and explicitly start camera tracking. First deny permission. Expected: guided mode remains reachable. Then grant permission and frame the full body. Expected: the camera starts only after your action, framing must become ready, and reps reflect complete measured movements rather than a timer. Compare a small comfortable set with a manual count; record discrepancies. Stop for pain or dizziness.
9. **Camera recovery and privacy.** Crop head/feet, introduce a second person, rotate the device, pause, background, return and interrupt the camera. Expected: counting pauses or asks for re-framing, accepted reps remain, and exiting releases the camera indicator. Try low light and a lower-end device for sustained use; record heat, lag and false counts. Raw frames/landmarks must not appear in exports. Public tracking stays off until these results are reviewed.
10. **Local reminders.** Grant notification permission, enable one daily and one weekly reminder near a convenient time, change a time, restart and return from background. Expected: one quiet notification at the configured device-local time, with no duplicates. Disable it and sign out; expected: old-account reminders stop. Deny/revoke permission; expected: settings explain delivery is blocked, without a false activation success. Repeat after a timezone change and reboot. Device focus/battery settings may affect physical delivery and must be recorded.
11. **App lock and accessibility.** Enable a lock, leave long enough to reach its timeout, and reopen through background/inactive transitions. Cancel and retry biometric authentication. Expected: the app remains locked until verification succeeds. Try large text, a small screen, dark mode and the keyboard. Expected: controls and important messages remain reachable, including save and Back.
12. **Export and doctor summary.** Export synthetic data as JSON/CSV/text, open the doctor summary and share it to a trusted test destination. Expected: the current account’s selected records and guided/measured workout labels are correct; no PIN, token, another account’s data or raw pose data is included. An unavailable backend must leave a visible error instead of an incomplete successful JSON export.
13. **Tracking-data deletion.** Delete tracked data from Profile. Expected: records disappear while the login remains; this action is not labeled full account deletion.
14. **Full account deletion — after candidate backend/rules deployment.** Export first if desired, then delete a disposable account with its current password. Expected: active stores and login are removed, other accounts are unaffected and the old account cannot sign in normally. On an injected service outage, expected: an accurate partial-failure message and a retry path, not success. A second device with an old session must not recreate records. Provider/backups/receipt retention follow the approved schedule, which is still pending.
15. **Legal and support.** During private QA, all seven legal pages must say they are unapproved drafts, with pending identity/contact/retention fields. Public release requires approved content, a functioning support channel and an external account-deletion request page. The founder approves these decisions; Codex performs the implementation and validation.
