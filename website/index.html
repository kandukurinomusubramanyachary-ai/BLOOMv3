Is Bloom a medical app?+

Bloom is a wellness companion for keeping track of your own experiences. It does not diagnose, prescribe, predict ovulation, or replace professional healthcare. Bring concerning symptoms to a qualified clinician.

What if my cycle is irregular?+

That’s part of why Bloom exists. The concept focuses on what you log over time, rather than fitting you into a 28-day schedule or assuming which phase you’re in.

Do I have to check in every day?+

No. Come back when it feels useful. Missing a day doesn’t erase what you’ve shared, and care should never become another source of guilt.

Is this the finished Bloom app?+

This is an interactive concept with fictional data. The real app is coming soon. Join the existing Bloom waitlist to hear when early access opens.

Where does my information go?+

Your sample check-in stays in this page and disappears when you leave. We remember essential preferences, and only keep campaign attribution if you allow it. Real signup takes place on the existing Bloom site, where you can review its questions and consent.
